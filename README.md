# Which Flies?

A seesaw weight game. Two things hover over a seesaw — Pokémon, pop-culture icons, animals, planets, black holes. Tap the one you think is lighter. The heavy one slams down and the light one gets launched, and the bigger the weight gap, the farther it flies: through the backyard, the city, the desert, the ocean, past the Moon and the planets, out of the galaxy, and beyond the observable universe.

**Play:** https://akashi7766.github.io/which-flies/

`index.html` plus the item list in `items.js` — no build step. Open it locally or serve the folder.

Art: [Twemoji](https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji) (CC-BY 4.0), Pokémon artwork via [PokéAPI](https://pokeapi.co), pop-culture and space images via Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons. Pokémon weights are official Pokédex values; fictional weights marked ≈ are fan estimates.
